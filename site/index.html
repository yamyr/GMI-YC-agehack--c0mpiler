Experience shouldn't retire.

Agely is an expert network where older adults turn decades of experience into paid, 15-minute sessions, and grow an AI engine of their own approved workflows.

Try the interactive demo

Why Agely

Judgment doesn't retire

A career builds judgment that is hard to find anywhere else. Agely lets older adults keep contributing and earning, without building a website or writing a sales profile.

Short, focused help

A founder facing an unfamiliar decision books a short conversation with someone who has handled that exact problem. Sessions are priced in 15-minute units.

People decide

Agents propose profiles, prices, matches and workflows. People confirm publication, booking and approval. Every match explains its reasoning.

How it works

  1. Margaret talks, the agent drafts

    Margaret describes a career in procurement, by voice or by typing. An agent turns the conversation into a profile she reviews, edits and publishes.

  2. She sets her price

    Agely suggests $60–$90 per 15 minutes and explains why. Margaret decides.

  3. Dev describes his problem

    Dev is preparing his first contract-manufacturing agreement. His agent drafts a brief and a budget for him to confirm.

  4. Agely explains the match

    Dev sees which of Margaret's skills fit and where their prices overlap.

  5. They agree on a session

    Thirty minutes at $75 per 15 minutes. Margaret accepts.

  6. Margaret's engine grows

    After the session, a debrief turns her approach into a reusable workflow, added only once she approves it.

Designed for older adults

Experts talk with an agent, by voice or by picking a suggested reply; typing is optional. Text is never smaller than 16px, buttons are large, and keyboard focus is always visible.
