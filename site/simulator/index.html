Agely Try the interactive demo

Run the marketplace

The demo follows one expert and one buyer. This simulator runs the same matching rule over a whole market: an expert fits a request when they list the skill and their price range overlaps the buyer's budget. Change the market, press play, and try to meet all three goals.

The people and numbers are made up. It shows how the rules behave, not what Agely will earn.
